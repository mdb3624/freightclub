## CHG-866: Narrow US-866 to Registration Only — No Password-Change Flow Exists

**Origin:** ARCHITECT's Input Acceptance Gate discovery pass on US-866 (Breached-Password Screening on Registration & Password Change), before design was finalized.

**What was found:** US-866's AC-2 assumes a password-change flow exists to attach breach-screening to. `grep -rn "setPasswordHash|passwordEncoder.encode" backend/src/main/java` confirms `passwordEncoder.encode()` is called exactly once in the entire backend — at registration, `AuthService.java:94`. There is no password-change endpoint, controller method, or DTO anywhere in the codebase. AC-2 cannot be implemented because its precondition (a password-change flow) doesn't exist.

**Root cause:** BA drafted AC-2 from the general "password creation or change" framing of the original request without an ARCHITECT/codebase check confirming a change flow existed to attach it to — a reasonable assumption that turned out false.

**Decision (Option A — finish story as-is, CHG tracked separately):** Narrow US-866 to registration only.
- AC-2 (password-change screening) removed from US-866's scope — nothing to attach it to yet.
- AC-1, AC-3, AC-4, AC-5 stand unchanged; all reference "registration" only going forward, not "registration or password-change."
- Password-change as a feature (endpoint, DTO, controller, service method) does not exist and is out of scope for this story — building it would be a large, separate, unscoped feature addition, not a narrow security-hardening fix.
- When a password-change flow is eventually built (its own future story), that story's AC should include breach-screening from day one, referencing this CHG and US-866's implementation as the pattern to reuse — not bolted on after the fact.
